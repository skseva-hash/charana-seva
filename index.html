⌚ Which watch do I have? · No watch?

Garmin, Polar, Wahoo: turn on the watch's heart-rate broadcast, then tap Connect.

Apple Watch / Galaxy Watch: they don't broadcast heart rate on their own. Install a heart-rate broadcast app on the watch (test yours before the event), then tap Connect.

Fitbit: not supported for live heart rate. You can still walk and appear on the leaderboard with GPS and steps.

No watch: borrow or buy a Bluetooth chest or arm strap and tap Connect, or use the camera pulse check. Distance, pace and steps work on any phone.
