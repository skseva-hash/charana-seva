Connect your registration form
Microsoft Forms

1. In Microsoft Forms, open your form, tap Responses, then Open results in Excel.
2. In Excel, tap Share, choose "Anyone with the link can view", and copy the link.
3. Paste it here.

If the link doesn't work, download the Excel file and upload it instead:

The list is kept only in this browser on this device. It is not sent to the database.

Google Forms

Link the form to a Google Sheet, tap File, Share, Publish to web, choose CSV, and paste the link here.

Anyone who has a published link can read the sheet, so keep it private.
